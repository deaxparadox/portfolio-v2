# Chapter 2
# Product Strategy & Visitor Journey

---

# 1. Product Objective

Portfolio v2 has only one objective.

Increase confidence.

Not:

- increase page views
- increase animations
- increase feature count

Instead:

Every interaction should increase confidence that I am an engineer capable of solving real-world problems.

---

# 2. Portfolio Funnel

Think of the portfolio as a confidence funnel.

Landing

↓

Interest

↓

Exploration

↓

Trust

↓

Conversation

↓

Opportunity

Every page should move the visitor one step deeper.

---

# 3. The Three Levels of Exploration

Portfolio v2 should support three depths.

## Level 1

Time

30 seconds

Goal

Understand:

- Who am I
- What I specialize in
- Where to explore next

Nothing more.

No information overload.

---

## Level 2

Time

5 minutes

Goal

Understand

- Experience
- Projects
- Resume
- Engineering ability

At this stage the visitor should think

"This person seems strong."

---

## Level 3

Time

30+ minutes

Goal

Understand

How I think.

Visitors should discover

- Architecture
- Engineering decisions
- Trade-offs
- Production issues
- Lessons learned
- Debugging stories

This level is designed for senior engineers.

# 6. Homepage Philosophy

The homepage is not the portfolio.

It is the lobby.

Its responsibilities are limited.

Answer only:

Who am I?

↓

What do I build?

↓

Why should you care?

↓

Where should you explore?

Nothing more.

The homepage should invite curiosity.

Not explain everything.

---

# 7. Card Philosophy

Every card answers exactly one question.

Examples

Experience

↓

Where have I worked?

Projects

↓

What have I built?

Engineering

↓

How do I solve problems?

Resume

↓

Need the ATS version?

Assistant

↓

Want a guided tour?

No card should attempt to answer multiple unrelated questions.

---

# 8. Content Hierarchy

The portfolio should progressively reveal information.

Card

↓

Overview

↓

Deep Dive

↓

Engineering Documentation

Visitors choose their depth.

The portfolio never forces it.

---

# 9. Engineering Over Features

Projects are evidence.

Engineering is the story.

Instead of saying

"I know Redis."

Show

Why Redis was chosen.

What problems Redis solved.

Where Redis became a bottleneck.

What would be done differently today.

The visitor naturally concludes:

"This engineer understands distributed systems."

---

# 10. Information Density

Avoid overwhelming visitors.

Every screen should answer one primary question.

Every click should reveal more detail.

Not more categories.

---

# 11. Feature Validation Rule

Before adding any feature ask

Does this increase confidence in me as an engineer?

If no,

the feature probably does not belong in Portfolio v2.

Examples

Chat

✓ if it explains engineering work.

Voice

✓ if it demonstrates AI capability.

Fancy particle background

✗

Random loading animation

✗

Terminal clone

Only if it provides actual value.

Otherwise remove it.

---

# 12. Design Goal

Visitors should leave thinking

"I would enjoy working with this engineer."

Not

"That portfolio looked cool."

That is the difference between a designer portfolio and an engineering portfolio.

---

# 13. The North Star

Portfolio v2 should communicate one idea.

Don't showcase the software you built.

Showcase the engineer who built the software.

Everything else supports that principle.
